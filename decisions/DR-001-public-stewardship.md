# DR-001: Public Stewardship

**Status:** Accepted for `v0.1.0-alpha.1`.

## Decision

Global AI Governance is the public steward of this repository. Stewardship covers release integrity, security response, compatibility policy, and maintenance of versioned public artifacts. The framework does not transfer institutional authorization to the steward or to repository contributors.
