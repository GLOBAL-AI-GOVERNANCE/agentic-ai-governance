# DR-002: Licensing Boundary

**Status:** Accepted for `v0.1.0-alpha.1`.

## Decision

Code, schemas, tests, utilities, and machine-readable fixtures use Apache-2.0. Specification modules, profiles, decision records, and prose documentation use CC-BY-4.0. `LICENSE_POLICY.md` is the controlling file-scope map.
