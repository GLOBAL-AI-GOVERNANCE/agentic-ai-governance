# DR-003: Assurance Boundary

**Status:** Accepted for `v0.1.0-alpha.1`.

## Decision

Alpha.1 validates declared artifacts and supplied evidence metadata. It does not observe production systems, prove factual truth, certify security, determine legal compliance, or grant institutional authority. Public claims and validator output must preserve this boundary.
