# Governance

Global AI Governance maintains this experimental public specification through transparent issues, pull requests, architecture decision records, and conformance evidence.

Normative changes require maintainer review, updated schemas where applicable, positive and negative tests, and a generated-specification consistency check. Alpha status does not imply external endorsement or certification.
Release-sequencing changes require an explicit, dated steward decision recorded in a governance decision record. Verification tooling may test that decision but may not silently create or supersede it.
