# Governance

Global AI Governance maintains this experimental public specification through transparent issues, pull requests, architecture decision records, governance decision records, and conformance evidence.

Normative changes require maintainer review, updated schemas and profiles where applicable, positive and negative tests, adversarial evidence for consequential changes, and generated-distribution consistency. Alpha status does not imply external endorsement or certification.

The root governance charter is operationalized through [PROGRAM_BASELINE.md](governance/PROGRAM_BASELINE.md), [VV_POLICY.md](governance/VV_POLICY.md), [RELEASE_GATE.md](governance/RELEASE_GATE.md), the machine-readable Claims Register, and prohibited-claims controls.

Release-sequencing changes require an explicit, dated steward decision recorded in a governance decision record. Verification tooling may test that decision but may not silently create or supersede it.
