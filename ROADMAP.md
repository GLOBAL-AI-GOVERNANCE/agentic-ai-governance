# Roadmap

## Alpha

- Expand cross-language RFC 8785 and signature vectors.
- Add implementation-independent conformance reports.
- Refine MCP, data-authority, and action-authority profiles.
- Expand the implemented Alpha.1 inventory schemas with cross-language fixtures and richer operational fields.

## Later

- Stabilize schemas after interoperability feedback.
- Define version-negotiation and migration guidance.
- Evaluate formal media-type registration and external review models.

- Extend the controlled Alpha.1 authority vocabulary only through versioned profile updates with new canonical policy anchors and adversarial regression fixtures.
